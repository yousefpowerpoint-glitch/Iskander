Printing it

  1. Press Ctrl + P (or ⌘ + P on a Mac) and choose Save as PDF, or send it straight to a printer.
  2. Set scale to 100% (not “Fit to page”) and turn on Background graphics, otherwise the dark back side prints white.
  3. For a print shop, hand them the PDF and ask for CR80 cards, double-sided, on 350 gsm matte card.
  4. Test the QR with your phone camera before printing a batch.
