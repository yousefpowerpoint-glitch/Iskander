Profile

Dr. Iskander Hasson A. Sattar

د. إسكندر حسن عبدالستار

Dr. Iskander Hasson A. Sattar is an Associate Professor in the Business Administration Section of the Faculty of Administrative Sciences at the University of Science and Technology in Aden, Yemen, where he teaches across the core subjects of the business administration programme, including its English-language track.

His research is applied and local by design. Most of his studies are field studies carried out inside real Yemeni institutions — banks, telecom operators, IT firms, food traders, sports clubs, humanitarian foundations, a government ministry and the universities of Aden themselves — asking how management practice shapes the results those organisations get.

Much of this work is written with his graduate students and colleagues, and he regularly sits on master’s thesis committees in business administration at the university as an internal examiner.
